How Sympto works
Tap tags each day
You'll create your own tags that describe how you feel — things like "brain fog", "wired", "heavy body". Tap the ones that apply each day.
Spot patterns over time
After a few weeks, the Patterns tab shows which symptoms tend to appear together — across all your conditions.
100% private
Everything stays on your device. No account, no server, no data leaving your phone.
